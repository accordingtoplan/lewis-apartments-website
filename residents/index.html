Common questions

Resident FAQs.

Log in to the resident portal at residentportal.lewisapartments.com to pay rent by bank transfer or card. Payments made before 5 p.m. PT on a business day are typically posted the same day.
We commit to responding to maintenance requests within one business day. Emergency maintenance (no heat, water leaks, locked out) is responded to 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Some restrictions apply; see your leasing office for full details.
The easiest way is through the resident portal, where you can submit a request, add photos and track its status. You can also contact your community's management office directly by phone or email.
We'll contact you 90 days before your lease ends with renewal options. You can accept, request different terms or tell us you'll be moving out, through the resident portal or your management office.
Pet policies vary by community. Most communities are pet-friendly with breed and weight restrictions. Contact your community office for specific policies, pet fees and deposits that apply to your home.
Most communities require 30 days' written notice. Check your lease agreement for your notice period, then submit your notice through the resident portal or in writing to your management office.

Our promise to you

The Lewis 24-Hour Service Commitment.

When something in your home needs attention, we take it seriously.

We commit to responding to maintenance requests within one business day, and to emergencies 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Some restrictions apply; see your leasing office for details.
