Who we are

Better communities.
Better lives.

Lewis Apartments is part of the Lewis Group of Companies, a family-led community development company founded in 1955 by Goldy and Ralph Lewis. What began as a homebuilding company in Southern California has grown into one of the nation's largest privately held community developers, with a legacy that includes more than 58,000 homes, 13,000 apartments and 24 million square feet of retail, office and industrial space throughout California and Nevada.


We take a community-first approach to development. A family-led organization devoted to excellence and service toward customers, employees, partners, investors and communities, Lewis takes a holistic approach to improving lives by delivering superior long-term value on investments, fostering genuine relationships, cultivating talent and supporting philanthropic efforts.
